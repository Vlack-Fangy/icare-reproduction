referencing revision `ce36a39eeb6da1bfb1f572730ad1f0cf0f68f02b`.

for one BLEU helper, TensorFlow NMT. These downloads remain outside Git. Their
licenses and notices remain applicable. Gemma weights are acquired separately
under Google's model terms. The MIT license here covers only original wrappers.
